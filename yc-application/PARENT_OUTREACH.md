# Parent Signal — Outreach + Interview Kit (48h)

Goal before submit: **5–10 parent conversations** with pain quotes + willingness to pay. Paste numbers into `ANSWERS.md`.

---

## Message template (WhatsApp / SMS / email)

Hi [Name] — I’m applying to Y Combinator for an AI tutor that teaches kids like a real 1:1 mentor (not another worksheet app). You’ve seen me mentor [child / kids]. Can I get 10 minutes today or tomorrow? I’m trying to learn what parents actually need. No pitch — just questions. Thanks!

---

## 10-minute interview script

1. How does your child feel about homework / reading / math right now?  
2. Have you tried tutors, apps, or YouTube? What worked / failed?  
3. When your child gets stuck, what do you see (shut down, anger, “I’m dumb”)?  
4. What would “a great private tutor at home” be worth per month?  
5. Would you let your child try a 15-minute session this weekend if I stay on the call?

## Consent for recording (say this)

“I’d like to record the screen of the tutoring session for a YC application demo. Your child’s face can be off-camera; first name only or a nickname. OK to proceed? You can stop anytime.”
