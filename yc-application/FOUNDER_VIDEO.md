# 60-Second Founder Video — Bullet Card

**Rules ([yc.com/video](https://www.ycombinator.com/video)):** founders talking only · ~60 seconds · no demo · no slides · no script reading · phone + window light is fine.

## Say this from bullets (do not read word-for-word)

1. **Who + what (10s)**  
   I’m [Name]. I’m building Primerycomb — an AI private tutor that teaches kids reading, writing, and math the way a devoted mentor does.

2. **Why you (20s)**  
   I’ve mentored kids 1:1 for three years. They keep coming back. What I noticed is tutoring isn’t content — it’s diagnosing *how* a child is stuck, repairing trust when they’re wrong, and remembering what clicked for them tomorrow. That’s what I’m encoding.

3. **What exists (15s)**  
   We have a working Mentoring Loop prototype — adaptive sessions, a persistent learner model, and a parent view. We’ve run early sessions with real kids [and talked to N parents].

4. **Why now / ask (10s)**  
   Models finally make tutor-quality possible at consumer scale. We’re starting with basic skills — the honest path toward something like the Primer. We’re applying for YC Fall 2026.

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Face camera, good light, clear audio  
- [ ] All founders on screen (solo = just you)  
- [ ] One take, natural, under 60s  
- [ ] Upload to YouTube unlisted or Loom; paste link in application  
- [ ] Demo video goes in the *separate* demo field — not this one  

## Upload tips

- YouTube: unlisted, no fancy intro
